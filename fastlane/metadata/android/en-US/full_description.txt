PocketUFW is a free and open-source, UFW-inspired inbound-only firewall for rooted Android devices.

One switch controls PocketUFW-owned IPv4 and IPv6 kernel rules that deny unsolicited incoming connections on supported physical network interfaces while leaving Android's outbound policies untouched.

Features:
* Verified protection switch and on-demand root checks
* Swipe navigation across Firewall, Activity, Insights and Settings
* Optional bundled Termux helper: four read-only views and confirmed pufw clear for owned counters
* Activity with physical/tunnel IPv4 and IPv6 counters and readable kernel rules
* Optional PINNED or DENY-ONLY inbound tunnel filtering
* Insights: on-demand, bounded DROP samples with source, protocol and traffic clues
* Optional one-shot Magisk restoration after reboot
* Tailscale incoming UDP listener discovery and manual refresh
* An active Quick Settings tile without periodic root requests
* No VPN slot, network telemetry, accounts, advertisements, or persistent packet history

Requires working root access (Magisk or compatible su), legacy iptables/ip6tables and a compatible Android kernel. No Google Play Services required.

This is beta software. It is not universal Android host protection. Only specified physical interfaces and explicitly enabled supported tunnels are filtered; earlier Android/OEM ACCEPT rules may bypass PocketUFW. Tethering and unsupported interfaces, including carrier IPsec inner interfaces, are not covered. Insights requires kernel NFLOG support; Android/OEM rule changes can cause a sample to be withheld after cleanup. Tailscale port changes may require an explicit Refresh Tailscale ports. There is no resident self-repair monitor.

Known Wi-Fi calling issue: incoming calls failed on a Pixel 9a with Android 17 after the carrier UDP 4500 connection-tracking entry expired. If you rely on Wi-Fi calling, do not enable physical filtering until this is fixed. Other phones/carriers and SMS are untested. PINNED verifies destination addresses, not cryptographic packet origin; tunnel filtering does not restore after reboot. Counter reset preserves rules, authorization and Insights samples.

PocketUFW is independent software, not affiliated with Canonical or UFW.
